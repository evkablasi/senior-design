As mentioned during our project briefing, SecurKart provides services for many different people and companies, including medical practices. As such, data protected by HIPAA and ERISA regulations exist in the same source control system which we will be using to work on our project. Building our product will not require us to work directly with any of this sensitive information, but it may lead to some critical decisions or additions being made that we otherwise would not have considered. 

## Summary
To ensure that our project does not mishandle any data, we first must define HIPAA and ERISA and the security laws that exist around them. The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) was created as a response to the growing use of technology for medical transactions and health information, and thus the growing need for privacy regulations [^1]. An amendment added in 2003, The Security Rule, goes into more detail about the responsibilities of companies to protect this information, which includes anticipating any possible threats or breaches [^2].
